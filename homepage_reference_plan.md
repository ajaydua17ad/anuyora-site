# ANUYORA Homepage Refinement Blueprint

## Core Objectives
- Retain professional, credible, trusted advisory-firm feel.
- Stick to the established color palette (Deep navy, refined blue, white, off-white) and typography (Playfair Display + Manrope).
- Keep existing responsive navigation, contact and enquiry functionality unchanged.
- NO new statistics, testimonials, certifications or unsupported services.

## Hero Section Refinement
- **Current Issue:** Detached rectangular image with airbrushed/faded edges.
- **Solution:** Recompose as one integrated photographic section. Replace the mask-composited small stock photo with a full-bleed or solidly structured edge-to-edge layout image.
- **Image Integration:** Utilize professional corporate imagery depicting a collaborative accounting team (Reference image provided in guidelines).
- **Audience Pathways:** Explicit clear paths for "Accounting & CPA firms", "Bookkeeping firms", and "Small & mid-sized businesses" via clear segmented CTA blocks integrated into the hero layout.

## Homepage Structure (5 Sections Maintained)
1. **Hero & Audience Segmentation:** High-impact integrated image with strong headline. Dedicated pathways for CPA firms vs SMBs right from the hero.
2. **Services & Capabilities:** Easy-to-scan grid layout of existing bookkeeping capabilities (no new services). Keep anchors active.
3. **How We Work:** Maintain the 5-step process but refine spacing and iconography for a more premium advisory feel.
4. **About:** Emphasize the trusted partnership model and US integration.
5. **Contact/Enquiry CTA:** Primary enquiry block seamlessly mapped to specific audience types.

## Design Adjustments
- **Typography:** Ensure `Playfair Display` is used for all major headings with crisp kerning. Use `Manrope` for legible body text.
- **Styling:** Remove `.hero-photo` CSS mask/gradient fading from `/app/frontend/src/index.css`. Use solid geometric container bounds.
- **Spacing:** Apply generous whitespace to establish a premium advisory look, avoiding cluttered BPO aesthetics.
